Who we are

A service club with a Fulton address

Lions Clubs International was founded in 1917 and has grown into one of the largest volunteer service organizations in the world. Our Fulton club is the local end of that: a group of people from Itawamba County who meet twice a month, pick projects that matter here, and get them done.

We're best known for vision — help for families who can't afford an eye exam or the glasses that follow. But the work goes wherever the need is: the flags on the holidays, and whatever the town asks us for next.

There's no special qualification for joining. If you live or work around Fulton and you want to be useful, you already meet the requirement.

What we do

Two things we keep showing up for

Every Lions club chooses its own projects. These are ours.

Glasses for kids

Help with the exam and the frames for children whose families can't cover it. A school nurse or teacher usually points them our way.

Flags around town

Members put the flags up on the holidays and take them back down again — the small jobs that keep Fulton looking like home.

Membership

Come to a meeting first

No application needed to visit. Show up on a first or third Monday, meet everybody, decide later.

Lions members are business owners, retirees, teachers, farmers, parents — people with an hour or two a month and a reason to care about this town. What you get out of it is a standing invitation to be useful.

  • Two meetings a month, one hour each
  • Pick the projects that fit your schedule
  • Annual dues cover club, district and international membership
  • Sponsorship by a current member is welcome but not required — just ask
